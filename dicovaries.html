Physical Sciences

Astronomy

  • Heliocentric Theory – The Sun is at the centre of the solar system.
  • Law of Universal Gravitation – Every mass attracts every other mass.
  • Expansion of the Universe – Galaxies move away from each other.
  • Discovery of Pluto – A new planet (at the time) in our Solar System.
  • Black Holes & Neutron Stars – Exotic objects predicted and later observed.

Physics

  • Laws of Motion – Explains how objects move and interact.
  • Electromagnetism – Unified electricity and magnetism.
  • Theories of Relativity – Changed our understanding of space, time, and gravity.
  • Quantum Mechanics – Behavior of matter & energy at atomic scales.
  • Discovery of Radioactivity – Revealed new kinds of energy & particles.

Chemistry

  • Periodic Table – Organized the chemical elements by properties.
  • Atomic Theory – Matter is composed of atoms.
  • Structure of the Atom – Discovery of the nucleus and electrons.
  • Synthetic Polymers and Plastics – Major industrial breakthroughs.

Mathematics

  • Calculus – A framework for change and motion.
  • Pythagorean Theorem – Fundamental relation in Euclidean geometry.
  • Zero and the Decimal System – Revolutionised arithmetic and computation.
  • Set Theory – Foundation of modern mathematics.
  • Non-Euclidean Geometry – Expanded the notion of space.

Earth Science / Geology

  • Plate Tectonics Theory – Explains movement of Earth’s crust.
  • Age of the Earth via Radiometric Dating.
  • Structure of Earth’s Layers – Using seismic studies.
  • Rock Cycle Concept – Interrelation of rock types through geologic processes.

Life Sciences

Biology

  • Cell Theory – All living organisms are made of cells.
  • Theory of Evolution by Natural Selection – Species change over time through selection.
  • Discovery of DNA Structure – Revealed the basis of heredity.
  • Mendel’s Laws of Heredity – Patterns of inheritance.
  • Photosynthesis Mechanism – How plants capture light energy.
  • Microscope and Discovery of Micro-organisms – Opening the unseen world.

Human Biology / Medicine

  • Circulation of Blood – How blood moves through the body.
  • Vaccination – Preventing disease via immunization.
  • Germ Theory of Disease – Micro-organisms cause disease.
  • Discovery of Penicillin – Revolutionised antibiotic therapy.
  • X-ray Imaging – Medical imaging breakthrough.
  • Human Genome Mapping – Sequencing the human genetic blueprint.

Botany (Plant Science)

  • Plant Cell Discovery – Understanding plant structure.
  • Plant Hormones (Auxins, Gibberellins) – Chemical regulators of growth.
  • Classification of Plants – Systematic organisation of plant species.

Zoology (Animal Science)

  • Theory of Evolution – Basis of animal diversity.
  • Classification of Animals – Systematic grouping of species.
  • Discovery of Animal Cell Nucleus – Understanding animal cell structure.
  • Genetics in Animals – Applying heredity to animal traits and breeding.

Microbiology

  • Discovery of Bacteria & Protozoa – The microscopic world.
  • Germ Theory of Disease – Micro-organisms cause illness.
  • Discovery of Viruses – Even smaller infectious agents.
  • Antibiotics – Medicines to fight bacterial infections.
